Most sites have hundreds of pages ranking on page one with almost no clicks. Here is the step-by-step method for identifying and fixing them quickly.

## Why CTR is the fastest win

Click-through rate is the gap between how often Google shows your page and how often people choose it. A page sitting at position 4 with a 1% CTR is leaving traffic on the table that a better title tag can capture in weeks, not months.

## The method

1. Open Google Search Console and go to **Performance > Search results**.
2. Set the date range to the last 3 months.
3. Filter by **Position** between 1 and 10.
4. Sort by **Impressions** descending.
5. Look for rows where impressions are high but CTR is low relative to position.

## What "low CTR" means by position

- Position 1-3: aim for 15%+ CTR
- Position 4-6: aim for 5-8%
- Position 7-10: aim for 2-4%

Anything well below these benchmarks is a candidate.

## Fixing the candidates

Rewrite the title tag and meta description around the actual query the page ranks for. Match search intent, front-load the primary keyword, and add a clear value proposition. Then monitor for 4-6 weeks.

## Measuring the result

Re-run the same report after 6 weeks. The goal is a measurable CTR lift on the targeted pages, which compounds into traffic growth without needing new rankings.
