Some people believe AI-generated results are making traditional SEO irrelevant. Here is why the technical foundation matters more than ever and what to prioritise right now.

## AI Overviews still cite sources

Google's AI Overviews pull from indexed pages. If your pages are not crawled, rendered, or indexed correctly, you cannot be cited. Technical SEO is the entry ticket.

## What matters most

- **Crawlability**: clean sitemaps, no orphan pages, sensible robots rules.
- **Rendering**: server-side or well-hydrated content that bots can read.
- **Core Web Vitals**: fast, stable pages get preferential treatment in both classic and AI results.
- **Structured data**: schema helps machines understand entities and relationships.

## What to prioritise right now

1. Fix crawl errors and redirect chains.
2. Improve Core Web Vitals on templates, not single pages.
3. Add schema markup for articles, products, and FAQs.
4. Keep content fresh and clearly attributed to real authors.

## The takeaway

AI changes how results are presented, not whether your site needs to be technically sound. The foundation is the strategy.
